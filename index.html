Help & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Explore comprehensive answers regarding our luxury ordering process, express shipping, secure payment gateways, and bespoke care instructions.

1. How do I place an order for a custom luxury piece? ↓
Simply browse our curated luxury catalog, select your preferred size (S, M, L), and click 'Buy Now' or 'Add to Bag'. Proceed through our encrypted secure checkout to finalize your acquisition instantly.
2. What payment methods are accepted at checkout? ↓
We accept all major secure payment gateways including UPI/QR codes, Credit and Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), Net Banking, and instant digital wallets processed via Razorpay.
3. How long does express shipping take? ↓
Since each piece is meticulously handcrafted, order processing takes 1-3 business days. Domestic express courier delivery takes an additional 3-7 business days. Complimentary worldwide shipping is active on orders above ₹1,499.
4. Can I track my luxury shipment in real-time? ↓
Yes! Once your parcel is dispatched from our master studio, you will receive an automated tracking reference via SMS and email. You can also track your shipment anytime via our dedicated 'Track Order' portal.
5. What is your return and exchange policy? ↓
We offer a 30-day hassle-free return and exchange policy for all unworn items in pristine, original condition with all tags attached. Contact our 24/7 customer support to initiate an instant courier pickup.
6. How should I care for my handmade crochet garments? ↓
To preserve the delicate weave and organic fibers, we recommend gentle hand washing in cold water with mild detergent or professional dry cleaning. Never wring or tumble dry.
